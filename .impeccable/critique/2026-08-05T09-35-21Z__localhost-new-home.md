# Design critique: /new-home before redesign

**Score:** 25/40. The page is usable and factually grounded, but it does not yet feel like one authored product experience.

## Heuristic assessment

## Design-specificity verdict

The page contains good local evidence but is category-interchangeable in its layout. Oswald headings, beige surfaces, black rules, and repeated two-column section intros do not express this workshop's distinctive combination of laser cutting, UV printing, engraving, and CNC production.

## Priority issues

- **P1:** The main page, order form, header, and footer read as different visual systems.
- **P1:** Contact and task description are not required before sending the new-home form.
- **P1:** The cookie notice obscures part of the first viewport.
- **P2:** The hero explains the offer but does not make production quality inspectable or memorable.
- **P2:** Technology, process, reviews, and FAQ repeat nearly the same composition.
- **P2:** Technology descriptions do not quickly translate equipment into customer jobs.
- **P2:** Mobile visitors have no persistent route back to the primary quote action.
- **P2:** The order form ends with several equally loud channels instead of a clear hierarchy.

## Preserve

- Verified dimensions and tolerances.
- Real production and portfolio photography.
- Clear support for one-off work and series.
- Existing analytics, routes, contacts, FAQ data, reviews, and structured data.

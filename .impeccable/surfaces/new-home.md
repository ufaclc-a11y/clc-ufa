# Surface brief: /new-home

- Mode: Persuade
- Audience: production teams, agencies, designers, and private customers in Ufa
- Job: understand which workshop capabilities fit the task and request a quote
- Primary action: calculate an order
- Secondary action: inspect real work
- Proof: real production photography, verified equipment limits, real reviews, contract and closing documents

## Selected direction

**Quality Control Light Table** (`Световой стол контроля`), concept seed `5efef862`.

The page should feel like a clear, well-lit production review surface: workpieces and processes are large enough to inspect; measured capabilities are placed beside the relevant technology; cobalt creates technical structure; laser orange appears only where attention or action is needed.

## Composition

- Full-bleed production hero with readable text over the image and one dominant quote CTA.
- Four technologies shown as alternating image-led bands, not equal cards.
- Work gallery uses an irregular editorial grid with captions outside images.
- B2B proof becomes a solid cobalt field with one process image and verified limits.
- Process becomes one connected route; reviews use one featured quote plus two supporting quotes.
- The final order area transitions into the existing dark footer.

No generated composition was available in this session. The user delegated execution, so the direction was selected from the local product evidence and existing real assets.
